點樣玩

How to play

數獨規則好簡單：每個數字喺同一行、同一列、同一宮（小格）只可以出現一次。

Classic Sudoku: each number appears once per row, column, and block.

  1. 1
    主頁揀玩法 Pick a mode on Home 「今日玩一題」用你最近一次輕鬆開玩嘅棋盤同難度；贏咗今日之後會變成「今日已玩」。「輕鬆開玩」可以隨時再揀 9×9／12×12 同簡單／中等／困難。 “Play Today” uses your last casual board & difficulty; after you win today it becomes “Played today”. “Play Casually” lets you pick 9×9 / 12×12 and Easy / Medium / Hard anytime.
  2. 2
    撳格再填數 Tap a cell, then a number 先揀空格，再撳數字鍵盤。題目畀定嘅深色數字唔可以改。 Select an empty cell, then tap the number pad. Dark given digits cannot be changed.
  3. 3
    用工具幫手 Use the tools 筆記、提示、檢查、標示相同、復原、清除都喺棋盤下面。開「認真解謎」就唔會即時震錯／計錯，用「檢查」先知。 Notes, Hint, Check, Highlight same, Undo, and Erase sit under the board. Focus mode skips instant shake/error counting — use Check instead.
  4. 4
    贏局同打卡 Wins & calendar 完成一題會記功業、最快時間，同埋開運進度。撳主頁日期可以睇月曆打卡（🏆 有贏、🚀 破紀錄）。 Finishing a puzzle updates Merit, best times, and Fortune progress. Tap the home date for the month record (🏆 win, 🚀 personal best).
小提示：復原唔會取消錯誤次數——錯咗就記低，鼓勵慢慢諗清楚再填。
Tip: Undo does not clear your error count — mistakes stay, so take your time.

遊戲工具一覽

In-game tools

全部都係大掣、清楚圖示，方便手指撳。

Large controls and clear icons, made for easy taps.

  • 復原Undo退返上一步（錯誤次數唔會減少）。Step back one move (error count stays).
  • 清除Erase清走目前格嘅數字或筆記。Clear the selected cell’s digit or notes.
  • 筆記Notes開咗之後填入嘅係候補數字，唔係正式答案。When on, numbers become pencil candidates, not final answers.
  • 提示Hint幫你填一格正確答案（有次數限制）。Fills one correct cell (limited uses).
  • 檢查Check彈出提示，話你知而家啱唔啱／有冇衝突。Shows an alert about correctness / conflicts.
  • 標示相同Highlight same高亮棋盤上同你揀嗰個一樣嘅數字。Highlights matching numbers across the board.
  • 認真解謎Focus關閉即時震錯同錯誤計數，專心慢慢解。No instant shake or error tally — solve at your pace.
  • 暫停Pause頂部播放／暫停圖示，計時會停。Play / pause icon at the top; the timer stops.
  • 收藏 ★Favorite ★遊戲中撳星星儲存；贏咗會自動移出收藏。主頁星星睇已收藏。Star a puzzle to save; winning removes it from Favorites. Home star opens the list.
