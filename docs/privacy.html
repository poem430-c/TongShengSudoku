Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Tong Sheng Sudoku (「通勝數獨日曆」) handles your information.

生效日期：2026年10月24日 · 最後更新：2026年10月24日

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. 開發者

1. Developer

本 App 由個人開發者提供。如對私隱有疑問，請電郵 poemcode.c@gmail.com。

This app is provided by an individual developer. For privacy questions, email poemcode.c@gmail.com.

2. 簡短摘要（畀 Apple／用戶一眼睇）

2. Short summary (for Apple & users)

  • 唔使開帳戶、唔使登入。
  • 遊戲進度同設定預設只存喺你部裝置（本機儲存）。
  • 我哋唔會出售你嘅個人資料。
  • 日後版本可能顯示廣告（例如 Google AdMob），同埋提供一次性／內購移除廣告；廣告合作方可能按其政策收集裝置廣告識別碼等資料。
  • App 唔使用面容／指紋、相機、咪、通訊錄、精確定位嚟提供核心玩法。
  • No account and no sign-in required.
  • Game progress and settings are stored on your device by default (local storage).
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Future versions may show ads (e.g. Google AdMob) and offer an in-app purchase to remove ads; ad partners may collect device advertising identifiers under their own policies.
  • The app does not use Face ID/Touch ID, camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location for core gameplay.

3. 我哋收集咩資料？

3. What information do we collect?

3.1 本機遊戲資料（存在你部機）

3.1 Local game data (on your device)

為咗令遊戲可以繼續玩同顯示成績，App 會用裝置本機儲存（例如 AsyncStorage）保存例如：

To keep your progress and stats, the app stores data locally on your device (e.g. AsyncStorage), such as:

  • 語言（EN／繁）、淺色／深色主題、「認真解謎」設定
  • 收藏嘅未完成題目、功業（勝場統計）、最快時間／最高分
  • 月曆打卡相關日子（有開局、有贏、破紀錄）
  • 「開運」進度、輕鬆開玩嘅棋盤／難度偏好
  • 是否已移除廣告（本機標記；正式內購接駁後會同交易狀態一齊處理）
  • Language (EN / 繁), light/dark theme, Focus mode setting
  • Saved favorites, Merit (win counts), best times / best scores
  • Calendar day marks (played, won, personal-best days)
  • Fortune progress, casual-play board/difficulty preferences
  • Whether ads are removed (local flag; once IAP is live, this ties to purchase state)

呢啲資料預設唔會上載去我哋嘅伺服器（本 App 目前冇用嚟登入／雲端同步嘅自家後端）。

This data is not uploaded to our servers by default (the app currently has no account or first-party cloud sync backend).

3.2 我哋唔主動收集嘅資料

3.2 Data we do not actively collect

  • 姓名、電郵、電話、住址等聯絡資料（除非你主動電郵我哋）
  • 精確地理位置
  • 相簿、通訊錄、咪高峰內容
  • 健康／財務帳戶資料
  • Name, email, phone, or postal address (unless you voluntarily email us)
  • Precise location
  • Photos, contacts, or microphone audio
  • Health or financial account data

3.3 廣告（計劃／日後版本）

3.3 Advertising (planned / future builds)

目前開發／試玩版本可能只顯示廣告位置佔位。正式上架版本計劃使用第三方廣告服務（例如 Google AdMob）顯示橫額或插頁廣告。 廣告供應商可能收集或接收例如：裝置類型、IP 位址、廣告識別碼（IDFA／AAID，視系統同你授權而定）、粗略地區、同廣告互動紀錄，用嚟提供同衡量廣告。 詳情請同時參閱供應商私隱政策（例如 Google： policies.google.com/privacy）。

Current development builds may only show an ad placeholder. Production builds are planned to use a third-party ad service (e.g. Google AdMob) for banner or interstitial ads. Ad providers may collect or receive information such as device type, IP address, advertising identifiers (IDFA/AAID, depending on the OS and your permission), coarse location, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads. See also the provider’s policy (e.g. Google: policies.google.com/privacy).

喺 iOS，如適用，App 會跟從 Apple 嘅 App Tracking Transparency（ATT）要求；你可以喺系統設定拒絕追蹤。 你亦可以透過內購（計劃中）移除廣告。

On iOS, where applicable, the app will follow Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) requirements; you can deny tracking in system settings. You may also remove ads via an in-app purchase (planned).

3.4 應用程式內購買（計劃）

3.4 In-App Purchases (planned)

「移除廣告」等內購會經 Apple App Store 處理付款。我哋唔會收到你嘅完整信用卡號碼；Apple 會按其條款處理交易。 購買狀態可能用於喺你部裝置解鎖「無廣告」體驗。

Purchases such as “Remove ads” are processed by the Apple App Store. We do not receive your full payment card number; Apple handles the transaction under its terms. Purchase state may be used on your device to unlock an ads-free experience.

3.5 診斷同崩潰報告

3.5 Diagnostics & crash reports

視乎你喺 iOS 系統設定入面有冇同意分享分析資料，Apple 或開發工具（例如 EAS／Expo 相關服務）可能提供匿名化或聚合嘅崩潰／效能資訊，用嚟改善穩定性。呢啲唔係用嚟追蹤你個人身份玩遊戲。

Depending on your iOS analytics settings, Apple or development tooling (e.g. EAS/Expo-related services) may provide anonymized or aggregated crash/performance information to improve stability. This is not used to personally track how you play.

5. 分享同第三方

5. Sharing & third parties

我哋唔會出售你嘅個人資料。資料只會喺以下情況交畀第三方： 廣告／分析／商店付款供應商（按其角色所必需）、法律要求，或者為保護用戶同服務安全。

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared with third parties only when needed for ads/analytics/store payments (as required for that role), to comply with law, or to protect users and the service.

6. 資料保留同刪除

6. Retention & deletion

本機遊戲資料會一直留喺你部裝置，直至你刪除 App，或者用系統方式清除 App 資料。 刪除 App 通常會一併清除本機儲存嘅進度。

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear the app’s data via system settings. Deleting the app typically removes locally stored progress.

This app is a general-audience entertainment product and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent/guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will help address it promptly.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect local settings and game data. However, no electronic storage can be guaranteed 100% secure.

10. App Privacy（App Store Connect 標籤參考）

10. App Privacy labels (App Store Connect reference)

填寫 Apple「App 私隱」問卷時，可按實際出貨版本核對。目前／近期預期大致如下（有廣告時請一併勾選廣告相關項目）：

When completing Apple’s App Privacy questionnaire, verify against the build you ship. Current / near-term expectation:

  • 本機遊戲資料／產品互動：用於 App 功能，與用戶身份無關，唔用嚟追蹤跨 App／網站（本機用途）。
  • 購買紀錄（如啟用 IAP）：用於 App 功能（解鎖無廣告）。

  • 診斷資料（如有）：可能用於 App 功能／分析以改善穩定性。
  • Local game data / Product Interaction: for app functionality, not linked to identity, not for tracking across apps/websites (on-device use).
  • Purchases (if IAP is enabled): for app functionality (unlock ads-free).
  • Advertising Data / Identifiers (if AdMob is enabled): may be used for third-party advertising; whether it is “used for tracking” depends on ATT and your ad setup — fill according to your final integration.
  • Diagnostics (if any): may be used for app functionality/analytics to improve stability.
